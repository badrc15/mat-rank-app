What Mat Rank does

Mat Rank lets fighters log remembered rounds after training. Partners can confirm, propose corrected counts, or close a recap as unverified. Pending recaps expire after 14 days without a rating change. Only mutually agreed wins and losses count towards Elo; rounds with no winner do not. Each partner pair has one recap per training date. It is not an official competition authority, medical service, coaching service or a way to decide whether two people can safely compete. Follow your club's safeguarding rules, qualified coaches and competition rules.
