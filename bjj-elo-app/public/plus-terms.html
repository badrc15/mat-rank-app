Stripe will process payments on its hosted checkout page. Mat Rank does not store full card details. Access begins once payment is confirmed by Stripe. You must use a verified account email. Cancel renewal

Use Mat Rank Plus → Manage subscription to stop future renewals. Access continues until the end of the paid period unless an immediate cancellation or refund applies. You can also email the operator with a clear cancellation request and the email linked to your account. Do not send card numbers or passwords. Account deletion stops future subscription billing before deleting your account; it ends access immediately and is not by itself a refund request. Deleting the app or signing out does not cancel a subscription.
