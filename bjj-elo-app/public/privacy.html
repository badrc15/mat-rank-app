Privacy Notice

Preview notice · version 2026-10-03. Controller: Badr Choudhary, badrc124@gmail.com. This release is for adults aged 18 or over. Public registration is closed while email delivery and launch arrangements are completed.

Who can use this release

Mat Rank is for adults aged 18 or over. Do not create an account for a child or share your account with one. We record your age confirmation without collecting your full date of birth. Please contact us if you believe we hold a child's information so we can assess and address it.

What we collect

Private verified email address, adult-age confirmation, account nickname, a salted password hash (not a readable password), profile details you enter (belt, weight, division and gym), after-training recap dates, proposed round counts, review status and confirmed results, Elo scores, training logs and streaks, likes, comments, account creation time and records of the terms accepted and privacy notice shown. We temporarily store pending signup information and hashed email-verification and password-reset tokens. We also store hashed session tokens and temporary hashed identifiers for request limiting. Hosting providers process connection data such as IP addresses and operational logs. Do not submit health information or children's identifying details.

Why we use it

We use account and match information to provide the requested ranking service and handle your requests. The lawful basis for adult account services, verification and recovery emails is performance of a contract. Security, abuse prevention and service integrity rely on legitimate interests, subject to a documented balancing assessment. Legally required records rely on legal obligation where applicable. Children are not permitted to register in this release. We must still assess the likelihood of children accessing the service and maintain proportionate safeguards. Accepting the terms is not blanket consent for data processing or marketing.

Who can see it

The current preview is gated by sign-in, but signed-in users can see nicknames, profile fields, results, rankings, comments and likes. Email addresses are private and are not included in the roster or match feed. Passwords and session secrets are not exposed through profiles or exports. Railway hosts the app in Amsterdam, Netherlands, with a persistent database volume. Resend is provisioned for account emails, but live email delivery is not enabled until a sender domain is verified. Backup restoration and operational arrangements remain under review. The operator must confirm processor contracts, any international transfers and applicable safeguards before opening registration. We do not sell personal data or add advertising or analytics trackers.

How long we keep it

Profile data remains while the account is active until deletion is requested. Session validity is seven days; expired session rows are removed by hourly maintenance. Rate-limit records expire after up to one hour and are cleaned by hourly maintenance. Terms acceptance records are held with the account. Deleting an account removes profile details and authored comments from the live database, but keeps completed match results under “Deleted account” for opponents; these may still be identifiable by context. Unverified signup and email-link requests expire after 24 hours; reset links expire after 30 minutes. Expired token records are removed hourly. Password changes and resets invalidate outstanding reset links. A verified production backup and deletion schedule must be established before launch. The operator still needs to approve and implement inactivity, retained-match, complaint and operational-log retention periods before launch.

Your choices and rights

Correct profile details in Profile. Use Account & privacy to download your information, change your password, sign out devices or delete your account. You may also request access, correction, erasure, restriction and portability, or object to processing where applicable, by emailing us. We may need proportionate identity checks, but will never ask you to email your password. Requests are normally due a response within one month, with lawful extensions where applicable. You can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Rankings and automation

Elo rankings are calculated automatically from mutually confirmed results and belt settings. Within each recap, decisive rounds use the same pre-confirmation ratings and their changes are summed because round order is unknown. Different recaps are applied in confirmation order. They are informal sporting estimates and do not determine access to employment, credit or legal rights. Contact us to challenge incorrect records. The privacy and wellbeing impact of ranking children must be assessed before launch.

Cookies and security

We use a strictly necessary sign-in cookie, described in the Cookie Notice. Passwords are salted and hashed with scrypt. Sessions expire and can be revoked. Production cookies require HTTPS and are not readable by JavaScript. Email verification and password recovery are implemented; availability depends on a verified sender domain. We do not currently offer multi-factor authentication. These controls reduce risk but do not promise absolute security.
