Cookie Notice

Version 2026-10-03 · Badr Choudhary · badrc124@gmail.com

Only essential cookies

Mat Rank uses a first-party cookie called matrank_session after you sign in or register. It keeps you signed in when you refresh or return to the app. It expires after seven days. Signing out invalidates it and removes it from your browser. Changing your password invalidates other sessions; “Sign out every device” invalidates all sessions.

The cookie is HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax and Secure in production. It contains a random session value, not your password. The server stores a hash of that value. The cookie is needed to provide the account service you request, so it does not depend on optional cookie consent.

No advertising or analytics

This version sets no optional advertising or analytics cookies and loads no third-party fonts or tracking scripts. There is no “accept all” requirement. If optional technologies are added later, they must stay off until an appropriate consent mechanism is implemented, with an equally accessible rejection and withdrawal choice.

Remembering this notice

When you select “Got it”, we store the notice version in local storage under matrank_cookie_notice to remember your requested dismissal. It remains until you clear website data or a new notice version is published. This is not marketing consent. Use “Cookie information” in the app to show the notice again.

Your browser controls

You can block or delete cookies in your browser, but account features will not work without the sign-in cookie. An obsolete local-storage sign-in token from earlier versions is removed when the app loads. No new authentication token is saved in local storage.
